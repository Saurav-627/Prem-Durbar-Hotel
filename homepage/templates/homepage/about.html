Heritage & Modern Hospitality

Our Story & Legacy

Nestled in Changunarayan-7, Nagarkot, Prem Durbar blends authentic Newari brick & wood craftsmanship with mountain tranquility, organic gastronomy, and high-altitude adventure.

Kathmandu Valley Artistry

Traditional Newari Architecture Meets Mountain Luxury

Prem Durbar was envisioned as a living tribute to Nepal’s rich architectural heritage. Every wall is constructed using traditional Newari red bricks, handcrafted stone masonry, and intricately carved wooden windows that echo centuries of Kathmandu valley royalty.

Overlooking the peaceful **Kamal Pokhari (Lotus Pond)** and surrounded by Nagarkot’s lush pine forests, our resort offers guests a tranquil retreat from urban life while keeping you connected to organic nature and panoramic Himalayan vistas.
